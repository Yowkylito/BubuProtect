# Hostnames the local DNS filter answers with NXDOMAIN, and counts against the app that asked.
#
# One host per line, '#' for comments. A leading '.' is not needed: matching is
# suffix-based, so "doubleclick.net" also covers "stats.g.doubleclick.net".
#
# Scope, because this list is doing two different jobs and only one of them is blocking:
#
#   - ATTRIBUTION. Every hit is recorded against the UID that asked, which is what turns "an ad
#     appeared" into "this app made 1,847 requests to 12 ad networks in the last hour". This works
#     whether or not the answer is blocked, and it is the more valuable half.
#   - BLOCKING. Only applied to apps the user has convicted. A device-wide block would break
#     legitimate free apps whose only revenue is the ad they were about to show, and this app has no
#     business making that decision on the user's behalf.
#
# Deliberately conservative. These are mobile ad-mediation and attribution networks, not a general
# tracker list - a maximal blocklist is somebody else's product and it breaks things.
